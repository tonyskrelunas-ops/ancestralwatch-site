Stone Breath · a private reading surface

Side by side

Every chapter twice — the edited one beside the one that is published

The left column is the chapter re-fused back toward your own sentences. The right column is the edition published through Draft2Digital on September 7 2026. 139 paragraphs differ across the twenty-one chapters. Nothing here is published, and nothing is indexed.

How to read it

Each chapter page opens with both columns. Changes only drops away every paragraph that did not move — chapter five goes from a hundred and nine paragraphs down to nineteen. Edited and Current give you one column at a time, which is the way to read it on the phone. Gold marks what changed.

Pick the voice

The same lines in five readers.

Each one over flute, drone, frame drum, wind and birds — synthesized here, the way the 1440 meditations were built. Every name in them is said the way you say it on your own recording: Masan, Tonas, Ada, Austėja, Ugnė, Rytis, Lukas, talka.
