The five ancestral voices

Five voices raised the boy who became the man — each carrying a different inheritance he would need on the world stage.

  • Masan — the Matriarch. The great-grandmother; the lamp the book is named for.
  • Vytautas — the Iron Wolf. The Lithuanian grandfather, keeper of the code of who they are.
  • Hastiin — the Singer. Herded into his nineties, lived past a hundred.
  • Ona — the Song Keeper. Keeper of the old songs of the forest.
  • The Warrior-Samurai — the Sword & Brush. Disciplined strength in service of the fragile.
