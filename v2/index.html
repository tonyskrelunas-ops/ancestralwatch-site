The premise

Every people on earth once raised far messengers.

Trained carriers who crossed deadly ground with what their people needed — and arrived whole. Not broken by the distance. Not emptied by the errand.

The tools were never lost. They were only set down. They can be picked back up.

Stone Breath

A love story about roots, wholeness, and a success that doesn’t cost your soul.

A Diné man arrives in a Lithuanian village carrying his grandfather’s book wrapped in a storm-pattern blanket — and a life that looks like success and feels like nothing.

The author

Tony Skrelūnas

Lithuanian & Diné

Raised in a hogan on Big Mountain by his great-grandmother. Learned to read by kerosene lamp.

He failed out of college. He came back and finished top of his class. That distance — out and back, broken and whole — is the road every book in this series walks.

  • PhD candidate, Sustainable Development Education, Prescott College
  • Dissertation on human & organizational performance through three lenses: Diné, Lithuanian, Japanese
  • Faculty at 1440 Multiversity
