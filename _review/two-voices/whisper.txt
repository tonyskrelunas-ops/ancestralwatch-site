== THE PHONEME CHECK, FEMININE VOICE (en-gb) ==
say_spellings.json was solved against espeak en-us for the masculine voice. The feminine voice (bf_emma) reads en-gb, so each spelling was phonemized again with espeak en-gb and compared with the en-us target after folding accent-only differences (əʊ/oʊ, e/ɛ, a/æ, ɒ/ɑ, ɜː/ɚ, the centring diphthongs against the r-coloured ones, an unsounded British r before a consonant). Where that changed the sound, lowercase alternatives were searched (as solve_say.py does). Result: tools/voice/say_spellings.en-gb.json.
138/140 names come out the same in en-gb. 1 spellings needed changing. The 2 that still differ, by one accent feature each:
   Yá’át’ééh    Tony: YAH-ah-TAY       en-gb says jˈɑːɹˈɑːtˈeɪ             target jˈɑː ˈɑː tˈeɪ            (not in any reading)
   Māori        Tony: MAH-oh-ree       en-gb says mˈɑːɹˈəʊɹˈiː             target mˈɑː ˈoʊ ɹˈiː            (not in any reading)
