### The Stone Breath
A 3-minute nervous-system reset built on an ancient running-and-breath technique. Use it before
            a hard conversation, after bad news, or any time the day gets loud. No theory — just do it.
- Choose your stone. In your mind, walk to a mountain stream and choose one pebble from the water. Feel its deep-time journey — the ages it took to come smooth. (Or hold a real stone in your hand.)
- Give thanks to the water. A moment of gratitude for the stream and all it has carried. Let your jaw and your shoulders drop.
- Breathe — five rounds. Unhurried: in for 4 · hold 4 · out for 6 · hold 4. The long exhale is the body's off-switch for alarm.
- Receive, then return. Hold the stone in your hand and imagine it on your tongue — never actually placed there. Receive the regard of those who came before. Then return the stone to the water, with thanks.
A few unhurried minutes — that's the whole practice. The stone goes in the hand and is imagined on the tongue, never actually placed there.
